# Business Analyst Career Research

## Role summary

A Business Analyst (BA) works with stakeholders to understand a need, define the
desired value, analyse the current situation, describe a feasible solution, and
help the team verify that the delivered change solves the right problem. The BA
connects business language with clear, testable delivery requirements.

## Core responsibilities

- Identify stakeholders and understand their goals, constraints, and concerns.
- Plan and conduct requirements elicitation through interviews, workshops,
  observation, document analysis, and prototypes.
- Define the problem, scope, assumptions, dependencies, and success measures.
- Model current-state and future-state processes.
- Write and prioritise functional and non-functional requirements.
- Convert needs into user stories and acceptance criteria that developers and
  testers can verify.
- Maintain traceability between stakeholder needs, requirements, implementation,
  and acceptance evidence.
- Support clarification, change control, demonstrations, and solution evaluation.

## Useful BA artefacts

- Stakeholder map or RACI
- Problem statement and scope
- As-Is and To-Be process models
- Product requirements document (PRD)
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Business rules, data definitions, assumptions, and decision log
- Requirements traceability matrix

## Applying BA work to the Week 1 MVC application

The Movie application has a simple stakeholder need: a user needs to maintain a
small catalogue without editing a database directly. That need becomes testable
requirements for listing, searching, creating, viewing, editing, and deleting
Movie records. Data validation expresses business rules such as required titles,
valid prices, and allowed classifications.
