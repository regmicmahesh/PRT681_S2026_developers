# Week 2 Business Analyst Research

## Role of a Business Analyst

A Business Analyst identifies business problems and helps stakeholders and
development teams agree on an appropriate solution.

## Basic Business Analysis Process

1. Understand the business problem.
2. Identify stakeholders.
3. Gather requirements.
4. Analyse and prioritise requirements.
5. Document requirements.
6. Validate requirements with stakeholders.
7. Support developers and testers during implementation.
8. Review whether the delivered solution meets the business need.

## Techniques Studied

- Stakeholder analysis
- Interviews and workshops
- Process mapping
- SWOT analysis
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria

## Reflection

I learned that a Business Analyst does more than write requirements. The BA
communicates with stakeholders, investigates the underlying business problem,
clarifies expectations and helps the technical team build the correct solution.
