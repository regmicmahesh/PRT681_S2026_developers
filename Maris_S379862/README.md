## Evidence boundary

The repository may demonstrate code, tests and written research. It cannot demonstrate attendance, meeting recordings, screen sharing, Teams communication, hours worked, installed software on another device or completed courses. The weekly evidence logs therefore include explicit fields for Maris to complete truthfully.
